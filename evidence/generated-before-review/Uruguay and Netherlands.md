# Uruguay and Netherlands

The Netherlands defeated Uruguay 3–2 in the first semi-final match played at the Cape Town Stadium on July 6, 2010, securing their qualification for the final for the first time since the 1978 World Cup.

## Details

- The Netherlands won the match 3–2.
- Giovanni van Bronckhorst scored for the Netherlands in the 18th minute.
- Diego Forlán equalized in the 41st minute.
- Wesley Sneijder scored for the Netherlands in the 70th minute.
- Robben scored for the Netherlands in the 73rd minute.
- Maxi Pereira scored a stoppage-time free kick for the Netherlands.
