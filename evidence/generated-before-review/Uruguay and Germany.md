# Uruguay and Germany

Germany defeated Uruguay 3–2 in the match for third place at the World Cup, securing third place for the second consecutive time. Uruguay nearly forced extra time when a 92nd-minute free kick by Forlán hit the bar, but Germany maintained their lead.

## Details

- Germany won the match 3–2 against Uruguay.
- The goals for Germany were scored by Thomas Müller (19'), Marcell Jansen (56'), and Sami Khedira (82').
- Uruguay's goals were scored by Edinson Cavani (28') and Diego Forlán (51').
- Uruguay almost forced extra time when Diego Forlán curled a 92nd-minute free kick onto the bar.
- The match took place on 10 July 2010 at the Nelson Mandela Bay Stadium in Port Elizabeth.
