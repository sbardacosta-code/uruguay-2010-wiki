# Costa Rica Playoff

The 2010 FIFA World Cup CONCACAF–CONMEBOL qualification play-off featured a two-legged tie between Costa Rica and Uruguay. Uruguay won the series 2–1 on aggregate.

## Details

- The play-off involved the fourth-placed team from the CONCACAF qualifying tournament, Costa Rica, and the fifth-placed team from the South American qualifying tournament, Uruguay.
- The first leg, played on November 14, 2009, in San José, ended with Uruguay defeating Costa Rica 1–0.
- The second leg, played on November 18, 2009, in Montevideo, resulted in a 1–1 draw between Uruguay and Costa Rica.
- Uruguay qualified for the 2010 World Cup after winning the series 2–1 on aggregate.
