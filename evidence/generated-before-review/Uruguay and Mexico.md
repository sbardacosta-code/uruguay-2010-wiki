# Uruguay and Mexico

Mexico played against Uruguay on June 22, 2010, at the Royal Bafokeng Stadium, resulting in a 0–1 victory for Uruguay. Luis Suárez was named Man of the Match.

## Details

- The match ended with a score of Mexico 0–1 Uruguay.
- The goal for Uruguay was scored by Suárez in the 43rd minute.
- The referee for the match was Viktor Kassai from Hungary.
- Uruguay's starting lineup included Fernando Muslera, Diego Lugano (c), and Diego Pérez.
- Mexico's starting lineup included Óscar Pérez and Cuauhtémoc Blanco (c).
- Luis Suárez was named Man of the Match.
