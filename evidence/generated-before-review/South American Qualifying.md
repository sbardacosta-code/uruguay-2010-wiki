# South American Qualifying

The South American zone of the 2010 FIFA World Cup qualification involved ten teams competing, with the top four teams qualifying automatically. The format mirrored previous CONMEBOL qualification tournaments, and the fifth-placed team entered a two-legged play-off against the fourth-placed team from CONCACAF.

## Details

- The qualification period ran from 13 October 2007 to 18 November 2009.
- Tournament statistics included 90 matches played, resulting in 232 goals scored, with Humberto Suazo being the top scorer with 10 goals.
- The top four teams that qualified automatically were Brazil, Chile, Paraguay, and Argentina.
- Uruguay, finishing fifth, participated in the inter-confederation play-offs.
- On 24 November 2008, FIFA suspended the Peruvian Football Federation from international competition, a suspension that was lifted on 20 December 2008.
