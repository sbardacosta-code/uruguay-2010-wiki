# Diego Forlan at the World Cup

Diego Forlán was recognized in the FIFA Dream Team, which was determined by an online public vote.

## Details

- Diego Forlán was listed as a forward in the FIFA Dream Team.
- The FIFA Dream Team featured six players from the Spanish team.
- The Dream Team included players from Germany, Brazil, the Netherlands, and Uruguay.
