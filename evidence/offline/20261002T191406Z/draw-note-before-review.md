# World Cup Group Draw

The FIFA final draw for the 2010 FIFA World Cup took place in Cape Town, South Africa, and determined the team groupings for the round-robin tournament style.

## Details

- The event, which included traditional African music and dance, was estimated to have been viewed by up to 200 million people globally.
- Jérôme Valcke and Charlize Theron announced the random selection of teams.
- Adidas' official 2010 Match Ball, Jabulani, was introduced during the ceremony.
- Group A pairings included South Africa, Mexico, Uruguay, and France.
- Group B pairings included Argentina, Nigeria, Korea Republic, and Greece.
