# Uruguay and Ghana

Uruguay and Ghana played on July 2, 2010, at Soccer City, Johannesburg, for a semi-final spot against the Netherlands. After a 1–1 draw in 120 minutes, Uruguay won the match 4–2 in a penalty shootout.

## Details

- The match was the first senior competitive football encounter between the two teams.
- Ghana took the lead just before half-time when Sulley Muntari scored from 40 yards.
- Diego Forlán equalized for Uruguay after half-time with a free kick.
- Luis Suárez was red carded in extra time after blocking Dominic Adiyiah’s goal-bound header with his hand.
- In the penalty shootout, Fernando Muslera saved the penalties of John Mensah and Dominic Adiyiah; Sebastián Abreu converted Uruguay's fifth kick with a Panenka-style chip to win the match.
- The final score after extra time was 1–1, with Uruguay winning the penalty shootout 4–2.
