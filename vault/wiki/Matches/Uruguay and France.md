# Uruguay and France

Uruguay and France played on June 11, 2010, at the Cape Town Stadium, ending in a 0–0 draw. Uruguay held France scoreless despite substitute Nicolás Lodeiro being sent off in the second half.

## Details

- The match took place on 11 June 2010 at the Cape Town Stadium.
- The final score was Uruguay 0–0 France.
- Uruguay's Nicolás Lodeiro received a yellow-red card in the second half.
- Zinedine Zidane criticized the French team and coach Raymond Domenech for a lack of teamwork after the match.
- Diego Forlán of Uruguay was named Man of the Match.
