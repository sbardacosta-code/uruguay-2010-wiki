# Uruguay and South Africa

Uruguay defeated South Africa with a final score of 3–0. Uruguay took the lead in the 24th minute, and the match concluded with Uruguay's third goal deep into injury time, marking Uruguay's first World Cup win since 1990.

## Details

- Uruguay scored their first goal in the 24th minute when Diego Forlán scored from 30 yards out.
- In the second half, a penalty was awarded against South Africa after Luis Suárez was brought down by Itumeleng Khune, who received a red card.
- Álvaro Pereira scored the third goal for Uruguay in deep injury time following a cross from Suárez.
- The match took place at Loftus Versfeld Stadium, Pretoria, with a referee of Massimo Busacca.
