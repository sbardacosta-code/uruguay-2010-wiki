# Uruguay and South Korea

Uruguay defeated South Korea 2–1 in the Round of 16 match, which took place at the Nelson Mandela Bay Stadium in Port Elizabeth.

## Details

- Luis Suárez scored two goals for Uruguay, with the second goal breaking a 1–1 deadlock in the 80th minute.
- Lee Chung-yong equalized for South Korea in the 68th minute with a headed goal following a free kick.
- Uruguay's victory allowed them passage to the quarter-finals for the first time since 1970.
- The recorded attendance was 30,597.
- Uruguayan coach Óscar Tabárez attributed the team's success to the experience of its players at top-level overseas clubs.
