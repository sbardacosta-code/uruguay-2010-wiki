# Group A Campaign

In Group A, Uruguay finished first with 7 points and advanced to the knockout stage, while Mexico finished second and South Africa third, both on 4 points. France finished last with 1 point.

## Details

- Uruguay finished in 1st position with 7 points, having played 3 matches, won 2, drawn 1, and lost 0.
- Mexico finished in 2nd position with 4 points, having played 3 matches, won 1, drawn 1, and lost 1.
- South Africa (H) finished in 3rd position with 4 points, having played 3 matches, won 1, drawn 1, and lost 1.
- France finished in 4th position with 1 point, having played 3 matches, won 0, drawn 1, and lost 2.
- Uruguay advanced to play South Korea in the round of 16.
- Mexico advanced to play Argentina in the round of 16.
