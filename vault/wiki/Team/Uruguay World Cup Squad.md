# Uruguay World Cup Squad

The Uruguay squad was coached by Óscar Tabárez and included players such as Diego Forlán, Luis Suárez, and Fernando Muslera.

The caps and clubs below are the source’s pre-tournament squad snapshot, not current career totals.

## Details

- Fernando Muslera, a goalkeeper, was listed with 6 caps and played for Lazio.
- Diego Lugano, a defender and captain, had 42 caps and played for Fenerbahçe.
- Diego Forlán, a forward, accumulated 62 caps and played for Atlético Madrid.
- Luis Suárez, a forward, had 30 caps and was associated with Ajax.
- Sebastián Abreu, a forward, had 56 caps and played for Botafogo.
- The squad included players from various clubs, such as Lazio, Fenerbahçe, and Atlético Madrid.
