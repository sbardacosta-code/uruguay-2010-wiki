# Diego Forlan at the World Cup

Diego Forlán scored five goals and was recognized as the tournament’s best player. He also appeared in the FIFA Dream Team, selected by an online public vote.

## Details

- Forlán recorded five goals and one assist in 654 minutes, according to the goalscorers section.
- Thomas Müller won the Golden Boot on the assists tiebreaker; Forlán’s best-player recognition is a different award.
- Diego Forlán was listed as a forward in the FIFA Dream Team.
- The FIFA Dream Team featured six players from the Spanish team.
- The Dream Team included players from Germany, Brazil, the Netherlands, and Uruguay.
